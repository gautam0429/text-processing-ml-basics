Edxso provides students with access to their enrolled courses through the student dashboard.

Students can view their course materials, lessons, and learning resources from the course dashboard.

Assignments must be submitted before the deadline mentioned on the respective course page.

Students can check their assignment status and submission details from their course dashboard.

Students can contact their assigned mentor through the mentoring section of the platform.

Mentoring sessions can be scheduled based on the available time slots shown in the mentoring section.

If a student cannot access their course or dashboard, they should contact the technical support team for assistance.

Students should use the email address registered with their Edxso account when contacting support.

Course-related questions can be discussed with the assigned mentor or submitted through the appropriate support channel.

Students can check their course progress from the student dashboard.

If a student misses an assignment deadline, they should contact their mentor or the relevant course support team to ask about the available options.

Students should keep their account credentials private and should not share their password with anyone.

Technical problems such as login issues, course access problems, or dashboard errors should be reported to the technical support team.

The availability of mentoring sessions depends on the mentor's available time slots.

Students should check the mentoring section regularly for available session slots.
